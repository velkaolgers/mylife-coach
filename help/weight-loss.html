Questions about hypnotherapy for weight loss

Can hypnotherapy help with weight loss?

Hypnotherapy can be a supportive approach for people who want to change their eating habits. Rather than a diet, it works with the subconscious mind, where cravings, emotional eating and old habits are rooted. Everyone is different, so during our first conversation we explore whether this approach is right for you.

Is this a diet?

No. I do not give diets or meal plans. Our work focuses on the thoughts, emotions and habits around food, so that the choices you already know are good for you become easier to make. For nutritional or medical advice, please speak to your doctor or a qualified nutritionist.

How many sessions will I need for weight loss?

It starts with a free 30-minute discovery call to get to know each other. Then we have an in-depth session, where I get to know your habits and your relationship with food, followed by a personal hypnosis session designed especially for you. Some people choose to continue with a few more sessions for deeper and lasting change.

Will I lose control during hypnosis?

Not at all. You remain aware and in control throughout the session, and you can open your eyes whenever you choose. Most clients describe the experience as a pleasant, deep relaxation, like the moment just before falling asleep, where everything feels calm and quiet.

Does hypnotherapy work online?

Yes. Hypnosis is about the state of your mind, not the room you are in. Many clients find it even easier to relax in the comfort of their own home. All you need is a quiet space, headphones and a little time just for yourself. Sessions are also available in person.
