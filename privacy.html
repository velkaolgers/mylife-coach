Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to me, and I want you to feel comfortable knowing how your personal information is handled when you visit my website, contact me, book a session, or work with me as a coaching or hypnotherapy client.

What information I may collect

When you contact me, book a discovery call or session, purchase a coaching or hypnosis service, or communicate with me, I may collect personal information such as your name, email address, contact details, booking information, payment status, and any information you choose to share with me.

During coaching or hypnotherapy, you may also choose to share personal information about your life, work, relationships, goals, emotions, wellbeing, or other topics relevant to our work together.

I only collect information that is reasonably necessary to communicate with you and provide my services.

How I use your information

I may use your information to:

  • communicate with you about inquiries, bookings and sessions;
  • provide coaching, hypnotherapy and related services;
  • manage appointments, payments and administration;
  • keep appropriate records relating to our work together;
  • send information you have specifically requested;
  • meet legal, tax, accounting or other regulatory obligations.

The legal basis for processing your information may include providing a service you have requested, fulfilling our agreement, your consent, complying with legal obligations, or legitimate business needs such as managing appointments and client communication.

Confidentiality

Information you share with me during coaching or hypnotherapy is treated confidentially.

I do not sell your personal information or share it for unrelated marketing purposes.

There may be exceptional circumstances where information must be disclosed, for example where required by law or where there is a serious and immediate risk of harm. Where appropriate and legally possible, I would aim to discuss this with you first.

Third-party services

To run my website and services, I use trusted third-party providers. These may include my website hosting provider (GitHub Pages), Cal.com for appointment bookings, Google Calendar and related Google services for bookings and communication, Brevo for my email list and newsletters, and Stripe for payment processing.

These providers may process limited personal information in order to deliver their services. Their own privacy policies and data-protection practices apply to information processed through their platforms.

Some service providers may process or store information outside the Netherlands or European Economic Area. Where applicable, they are responsible for using appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

How long I keep your information

I keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for providing my services, maintaining appropriate client records, or meeting legal, tax and administrative requirements.

When information is no longer needed, I will delete it or securely dispose of it where reasonably possible.

Your privacy rights

Under applicable data-protection law, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information I hold about you;
  • request access to your information;
  • ask for incorrect information to be corrected;
  • request deletion of your information where legally possible;
  • ask me to restrict certain uses of your information;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

If you would like to exercise any of these rights, simply contact me using the email address provided on this website.

You also have the right to raise a concern with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, if you believe your personal information has not been handled appropriately.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact me through the contact details provided on this website.
