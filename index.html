My name is Velka Olgers, and I am a transformational life coach and hypnotherapist.

I started incorporating these new insights into my work, first as a young teacher, then as a human resources specialist at an IT company, later as an entrepreneur in an investment company in Bulgaria, and last but not least, in my role as a mother and wife.

A few years ago, I decided it was time to build on this purpose and focus on what I do best. I started coaching people professionally and continued studying to gain new insights and master different, more impactful methods.

Today, I am an IAPCP-certified and accredited hypnotherapy practitioner, a Transformational Life Coach, and a certified NLP Practitioner trained in both Classic and New Code NLP through the International Trainers Academy of NLP. Over the years, I have continued to expand my expertise, bringing together hypnotherapy, Timeline NLP, and other powerful approaches to help my clients create meaningful, lasting change.

I am proud to work with a diverse group of clients from around the world, representing many different backgrounds and life experiences.

FAQ & Answers

Life coaching

What is the difference between coaching and therapy?

Therapy often looks back, to heal what happened in the past. Coaching looks forward, to where you want to go and what is holding you back from getting there. Together we create clarity, set meaningful goals and take the steps that bring you closer to the life you truly want.

Who is life coaching for?

Coaching is for anyone who feels ready for a next step, whether that is more confidence, a new direction in your career, or simply feeling more like yourself again. Many of my clients are successful professionals who sense there is more waiting for them, and want support to reach it.

How many sessions will I need?

Some clients find the clarity they were looking for in a single session. For deeper and lasting change, a series of sessions gives you the time to grow, practice and integrate what you discover. We can decide together what feels right for you during your free discovery call.

Visualization

What is visualization?

Visualization is the art of creating a clear, vivid picture of the future you desire, and stepping into it as if it were already real. When you see it, hear it and feel it, your mind begins to recognize this future as a direction to move toward.

How does visualization work?

Every time you imagine your goal with feeling, you strengthen the connection between your vision and your actions. Your mind starts to notice opportunities, ideas and choices that support where you want to go. Visualization guides the way, and your steps make it happen.

What if I can’t “see” clear pictures in my mind?

That’s completely fine, and more common than you might think. Some people see images, others hear words, sense a feeling or simply know. There is no wrong way to visualize, and I will guide you in the way that works naturally for you.

Hypnotherapy

What is hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy is a natural, deeply relaxed state of focused attention, in which we gently work with your subconscious mind. In this state, we are tapping into the unlimited resources of the subconscious mind, where old patterns can be released and new, supportive ones can take their place.

Will I lose control during hypnosis?

Not at all. You remain aware and in control throughout the session, and you can open your eyes whenever you choose. Most clients describe the experience as a pleasant, deep relaxation, like the moment just before falling asleep, where everything feels calm and quiet.

Does hypnotherapy work online?

Yes. Hypnosis is about the state of your mind, not the room you are in. Many clients find it even easier to relax in the comfort of their own home. All you need is a quiet space, headphones and a little time just for yourself. Sessions are also available in person.

What can hypnotherapy help me with?

Many clients come to me when they feel stuck in a pattern they can’t change by willpower alone. This could be a lack of confidence, stress and anxious thoughts, weight loss, sleep and insomnia, or habits that no longer serve them. Hypnotherapy works with the subconscious mind, where these patterns live, which is why change can feel surprisingly natural once it begins.

Which techniques do you use in your sessions?

Every session is tailored to you. Depending on what you need, I combine hypnotherapy with regression, to gently understand where a pattern began, and with inner child reimprinting, to bring comfort and new resources to a younger part of you. I also use Timeline techniques, to release the emotional weight of past experiences and create a more empowering path forward.

Can hypnotherapy support healing of body and mind?

Mind and body are deeply connected, and how we feel inside often shows up in the body. Hypnotherapy can be a supportive, complementary approach alongside medical care, for example in coping with pain, stress-related complaints or the emotional impact of difficult past experiences. It never replaces a doctor or psychologist, but it can help you feel calmer, stronger and more in touch with your own healing resources.
